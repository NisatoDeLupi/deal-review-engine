# Xoxoday — Working Brand Guidelines (for decks & internal assets)

> Derived from the public Xoxoday website (xoxoday.com, June 2026) — brand story, values, product
> system and visual tone. Exact hex values below are a faithful working palette, not the official
> brand kit. Confirm against the official assets at
> `xoxoday.com/downloads/xoxoday-brand-assets-usage-guidelines` before external use.

## Brand in one line
Xoxoday is an AI-first rewards, loyalty and incentives platform. The brand voice is **warm, human
and celebratory**, but the surface is **enterprise-grade and confident**. Tagline energy: *"Make
every relationship rewarding."* Lead value: *"Human first."*

## Color palette

A restrained palette: deep plum-ink and light neutrals carry ~70% of every layout, coral is the
single hero accent, plum is the secondary, gold is reserved for standout numbers.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| Base / ink | Plum Ink | `#1B1530` | Dark backgrounds, body text on light, titles |
| Panel (dark) | Deep Plum | `#2A1E47` | Cards/panels on dark slides |
| Hero accent | Xoxoday Coral | `#F4593F` | Kickers, emphasis, lines, arrows, CTAs |
| Accent light | Coral Light | `#FB7A63` | Hover/secondary coral, subtle fills |
| Secondary | Plum | `#7A3D9C` | Secondary panels, "Ask the room" banner, alt headers |
| Highlight | Empuls Gold | `#FFBC2E` | Big stat numbers, single points of delight |
| Surface | Cloud | `#F6F4FA` | Light slide backgrounds (cool, slight plum tint — never cream) |
| Card | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on light slides |
| Muted (light) | Slate | `#6B6480` | Secondary text on light |
| Muted (dark) | Ice | `#D9D2EA` | Secondary text on dark |

**Dominance rule:** one hue dominates per slide. Coral never competes with gold — gold appears once
per slide at most, on a number.

## Typography

| Element | Font | Size (deck) |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Display / headers | **Trebuchet MS** (humanist sans — friendly + modern, fits "human first") | 30–50 pt bold |
| Kicker / label | Trebuchet MS | 12–13 pt bold, letter-spaced, uppercase |
| Body | **Calibri** | 13–17 pt |
| Caption / footnote | Calibri italic | 12–14 pt |

Pairing principle (from the design skills): one header font with personality, one clean body font —
never more than two families.

## Layout & motif

- **Sandwich structure:** dark plum-ink for title / section / closing slides; light Cloud for content.
- **Motif:** thin concentric rings (coral / plum / gold) bleeding off corners on dark slides; a
  6 px coral edge-bar on cards. Repeat the same motif on every slide — don't restyle one and leave
  the rest plain.
- **Engagement motif:** a plum "Ask the room" pill with a coral `?` badge marks every audience question.
- **Whitespace:** 0.5" minimum margins; 0.3–0.5" between blocks. Leave room to breathe.
- **Avoid (AI-slop tells):** no underline accent rules beneath titles, no full-width colored header
  bars, no cream backgrounds, no text-only slides — every slide carries a visual element.

## Product sub-brands (for reference)
- **Plum** — rewards, payouts, catalog (plum/purple).
- **Empuls** — employee engagement & recognition (white + gold).
- **Loyalife** — customer loyalty & referrals.

## Voice notes
Direct, concrete, human. Celebrate outcomes, not features. "Strong opinions, loosely held."
Avoid hype; let the numbers and the story carry weight.
