# Xoxoday — Brand voice, messaging & product one-liners

Use this to keep copy on-brand. Pair with the color/type system in `SKILL.md`.

## Voice
Warm and human, but enterprise-grade and confident. Direct and concrete. Celebrate outcomes, not
features. Lead with proof. Avoid hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "synergy"). Strong
opinions, loosely held. Write the way a sharp, senior peer would brief you — short sentences, real numbers.

## Positioning (master)
The **AI-first platform for rewards, incentives, loyalty and payouts** — one modular enterprise
platform to engage employees, motivate sales teams, retain customers, activate partners, and
automate global reward operations.

- SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, CCPA; RBAC; cloud / private / on-sdr_o deployment

recommendations, predictive analytics (attrition/churn/quota/CLTV), smart nudges, engagement

## Audience hooks (for battle cards / decks)
- HR / People: culture, recognition, retention, total rewards, tax-saving benefits.
- Sales leaders: commission automation, quota planning, instant payouts, gamified leaderboards.
- Marketing / Loyalty: points/tiers, card-linked offers, referrals, CLTV, segmentation.
- Channel: dealer/distributor loyalty, agent incentives, global payouts in 100+ currencies.
- Procurement / IT: TCO, security/compliance, deployment flexibility, API-first.

## Copy do/don'ts
- DO quantify ("700+ rewards in 45 days, zero API downtime"). DON'T vague-claim ("best-in-class").
- DO name the relationship (employees, sales, partners, customers, merchants).
- DON'T attribute fake quotes to real customers; use only verified testimonials.
