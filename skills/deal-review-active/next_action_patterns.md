# Next Action Patterns

Guidance for writing the "Recommended Next Action" line on each deal. The standard for this skill is execution-level specificity: name a person, a deliverable, and a timeline. Vague actions like "follow up with customer" are not acceptable.

## Pattern by scenario

### Large deal missing AE summary
Pattern: `URGENT: <AE> to provide full summary today/tomorrow — <reason>. Define closure timeline + blockers. Worth executive (CEO/CPO) involvement if needed.`

Example: Account_099 (₹15.5L Proposal, zero notes)
> URGENT: AE_27 to provide full summary today/tomorrow — this is the largest deal in the pipeline. Define closure timeline + blockers. Worth executive (CEO SDR_M/CPO Person_56) involvement if needed.

### Stalled commercial / proposal not sent
Pattern: `<AE> to send formal proposal at <agreed numbers> THIS WEEK. <Open question to resolve>. Push for signed by <date>.`

Example: Account_100 Loyalife (pricing decided Feb 26, proposal not sent)
> AE_13 to SEND THE PROPOSAL THIS WEEK. This is the largest Loyalife/LBMS deal in pipeline — critical to advance to Proposal stage. June close achievable if proposal goes out now.

### Dead POC / left the company
Pattern: `<AE> to <one-call action with new contact>. If no response in <X days/weeks>, MARK CLOSED-LOST. <ACV justification>.`

Example: MOL (POC Person_06 left, new contact Person_26 given)
> AE_27 to ONE-CALL Person_26. If no response, MARK CLOSED-LOST. 2-year-old deal at small ACV doesn't deserve more time.

### Should be closed-lost (currently in advanced stage)
Pattern: `MARK CLOSED-LOST. <Reason>. Should not be in <current stage>. Move and free up pipeline.`

Example: INTECH (POC left, no budget, but sitting in Proposal)
> MARK CLOSED-LOST. POC has left, no budget. Should not be in Proposal stage. Move and free up pipeline.

### Strong buying intent, ready to close
Pattern: `<AE> to send <specific deliverable> THIS WEEK. <Customer commitment / signal>. <Realistic close date>.`

Example: Account_244 (Loyalife, customer will finalize in 10 days)
> AE_13 URGENT: send commercials + POS integration flow + offline business case studies THIS WEEK. They'll finalize plan in 10 days. Strong June close candidate.

### Long silence / 30+ days no activity
Pattern: `<AE> to reach <POC> THIS WEEK with <fresh angle>. If no response in <X weeks>, closed-lost.`

Example: Account_260 (no activity, demo done long ago)
> AE_27 to reach Person_20 THIS WEEK with a value-pitch refresh. If no response in 2 wk, closed-lost.

### Email trail reveals unfulfilled commitment
Pattern: `Email trail shows <commitment from rep or customer>. <Specific delivery action>. <Timeline>.`

Example: Customer asked for proposal Apr 12, never sent
> Email trail shows customer asked for proposal Apr 12 — still not sent. AE_13 to send proposal at agreed numbers THIS WEEK with apology for delay. Confirm signing timeline.

### Ghosted / one-way email activity
Pattern: `<N> outbound emails, <M> inbound since <date>. Customer has gone silent — <decision>.`

Example: Many outbound, zero inbound since Feb
> 12 outbound emails, 0 inbound since Feb. Customer has ghosted — AE_27 to make one final phone attempt + LinkedIn ping. If no response in 1 week, MARK CLOSED-LOST.

### No call attempts in 60+ days
Pattern: `Last call was <date> (<disposition>). <N> days silent. <Action> this week.`

Example: Last call Mar 8, no-answer, deal in Negotiation stage
> Last call was Mar 8 (no-answer). 60 days silent on a Negotiation-stage deal is unacceptable. AE_13 to call POC THIS WEEK + log outcome. Decide rescue vs closed-lost.

### Call notes reveal pending dependency
Pattern: `Call notes from <date> mention <dependency>. Chase that thread + <action>.`

Example: Apr 22 call mentioned CHRO approval pending
> Call notes from Apr 22 mention CHRO approval pending. SDR_Q to follow up with POC for CHRO meeting date + offer to join the pitch. Close target end-May.

### High email volume but no recent calls
Pattern: `<N> emails but 0 calls in last <period>. Pick up the phone — <reason>.`

Example: 8 emails back-and-forth but no calls in 45 days
> 8 emails back-and-forth but 0 calls in 45 days. Email-only conversations stall in this stage. AE_27 to schedule a 30-min call THIS WEEK to accelerate.

Example: Account_065 (Deputy Manager POC)

### Owner transition / KT needed
Pattern: `<Old AE>+<New AE> KT call this week. <New AE> to <re-engagement action>. Update close date. Decision: revive vs closed-lost within <X weeks>.`

Example: Account_182 (transferred AE_13 → AE_74, phone dead)
> AE_13+AE_74 call this week. AE_74 to re-establish contact via LinkedIn/email (since phone was dead). Update close date. Decision: revive vs closed-lost within 2 weeks.

### Partner-dependent deal
Pattern: `<AE> + <Partnership owner> to chase <partner> for <partner deliverable>. Reset close to <date>. Co-dependency = risk.`

Example: Account_061 (Empuls bundled with Account_196 deal)
> AE_13 + AE_46 to chase Person_24 for Account_196 deal closure status. Reset Empuls close to June. Co-dependency = risk.

### Competitive evaluation underway
Pattern: `<AE> to send competitive battlecard vs <competitor1>/<competitor2> + <case studies>. Reset close to <date>. <Confidence statement>.`

Example: Account_019 (evaluating vs Advantage Club + Bonusly)
> AE_13 to pull SMB case studies and customer references THIS WEEK. Update close date to July (post their budget cycle). Strong against Advantage Club + Bonusly with right proof.

### Small ACV / low-touch
Pattern: `<AE> to <one specific action>. Don't over-invest given low ACV (<amount>).`

Example: Account_225 Welcome Kit (₹20K)
> SDR_Q to chase shortlisting + close small deal. Don't over-invest given low ACV.

### Missed close date
Pattern: `<AE> to <action>. Update close date to <realistic new date>. <Risk assessment>.`

Example: Account_125 (close target May 31 with no activity)
> AE_13 to confirm if POC call happened, share recap. Close target May 31 — need to push proposal acceptance THIS WEEK.

## Rules for action writing

1. **Name the person** — "AE_13" not "the AE", "Person_20" not "the POC"
2. **Specify the deliverable** — "send formal proposal at 4.5L + 50K setup" not "share commercials"
3. **Timeline always** — "THIS WEEK", "by EOW", "by end-May", "in 2 weeks" — never open-ended
4. **Include the trigger to kill** — "if no response in X days, closed-lost" gives the AE a decision deadline
5. **Reference past commitments** — "March commitment missed" reminds the rep of accountability
6. **Surface escalation paths** — when CEO/CPO involvement is warranted, say so explicitly
7. **Avoid hedging language** — "should", "could", "might" weaken the action. Use imperatives.

## Length

Aim for 1-3 sentences. The note already contains the deal summary and manager context. The action line is the bottom-line "what happens next."

Too short: "Follow up with POC." (vague, no accountability)
Too long: 4+ sentences become a wall of text that gets skimmed past.
Right: "AE_13 to send revised commercial at 4.5L + 50K setup THIS WEEK. Confirm tech compatibility for direct bank cashback. Push for signed by end-May."
